Reading this tracker: Chaptered bills are law; a vetoed bill’s proposed requirements did not become law. County impact colors run from red for a Development Code update to green when no Planning change is identified. Purple means the impact needs review. The workbook review covers June 2025 onward, including the May 21–June 9 overlap, the 2025 signed and enrolling sheets, the 2026 sheets and working list. Opening the tracker starts a source scan if the last one was at least 24 hours ago. “Update tracker” starts a scan at any time. Scans check public daily updates, rotate through keyword searches and known bills, and save unprocessed candidates for later scans. Broad titles are checked against official bill text. Newly discovered bills wait for your approval. Removed bills stay in their own list and are skipped by future dashboard scans until you add them back. Permanently deleted bills disappear from the dashboard and remain blocked from scans. Keyword searches show only their first results page; this is not a complete search of every bill.
